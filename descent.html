DEATH ENTHRALLED

Following the disappearence of an aspiring bassist of an infamous band, a pair of detectives from a police district—known to contain and document strange sightings and superstitions—called the Pretenatural Corporeal Sentries Department, are assigned to investigate the cause of it.

While their investigation seems innocent and serene on the surface, the two find out the sleepy town of Brimstone Peaks wields something bigger: when familiar roads become strange, and the crows call, a harrowing darkness lurks on the horizon.
